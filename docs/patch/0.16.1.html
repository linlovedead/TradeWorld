After 0.16 notchris12 sent a detailed list of seven points, and then one more comment: "Best market" sells only in very low amounts. That was right word for word — we checked the code. All of it is in 0.16.1. Thank you!

From your feedback

  • ✚ Many buildings at once: in the corporation's "Management" tab — upgrade them all, add a machine stage to every line, repair the worn lines. Every button shows its full cost.
  • ✚ The country's power grid: its own panel on the "Country" tab — capacity, demand, load, the price of power and why, outages and what your buildings pay.
  • ✚ The exchange is no longer instant: a lot you buy travels to the warehouse by logistics, a lot you sell is paid when it reaches the buyer. Within one province it stays instant. Goods on the way and money due count in your net worth, and the "Logistics" window shows what you are owed.
  • ✚ "Best market" really sells now: a line used to take the five dearest countries — small and far, and some of them paid less than home after shipping. Now it takes ten markets by how much they actually add a month, checks the list every week and sees a route change, a war or a closed strait right away. In our test the share sent abroad grew from 22% to almost half of what the markets paying more can take.
  • ✚ Children: the chance falls step by step with the mother's age up to 47 instead of stopping at 44; the family window lets you adopt a child of 1–6 (once a year, from personal money).
  • ✚ Talents have icons — beside the name and inside the tier circles, as in CK3.

The world of 2000

  • ✚ The seventeen countries with a detailed history now start as they were in 2000, not as they are today. Russia after the default, with the war in Chechnya and a new president, in the G8; China before the WTO, with tight currency controls; Chávez's Venezuela of 2000 with a free press; Syria under the Baath party; Niger with a president elected in 1999. Laws, relations, alliances and descriptions follow the verifiable facts of the time, without judging "good" or "bad".
  • ✚ Invented territorial claims between countries with no common border are gone.
  • This applies to new games: a loaded save keeps its world as it was.
