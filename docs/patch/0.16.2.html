0.16.2 is about computing and energy. The game has a whole new chain: power → data centre → the cloud and tokens. It all runs by the same rules as the rest of the world: cloud and AI companies appear on their dates and compete with you on the same market.

Computing

  • ✚ Data centres: servers are a good made from chips, a data centre holds racks, and the main cost is power; cooling is cheaper in the cold and dearer in the tropics. Your platforms run on your own capacity.
  • ✚ The cloud from 2006: the world's market takes your spare capacity; the price falls every year.
  • ✚ AI models from 2022: train your own model and sell tokens through its API against the world's AI companies. Each generation is four times the work. Thanks to Patient glitch for the idea of tokens as a chain of their own.
  • ✚ The IT office: teams work like a plant's lines. Games are titles of their own: genre, setting, platform, the critics' score, sales, sequels. The world's studios make games by your rules, and the critics get used to good games.

Power and the state

  • ✚ Your own power stations: gas, coal, hydro, solar, wind, nuclear. A station sells to the country's grid at the grid's price.
  • ✚ Power between countries: neighbouring grids trade, the cheap one sells to the dear one, and both treasuries gain. The grid window shows capacity, load, the price per MWh and three years of history.
  • ✚ The country's budget month by month, and a head of state sees the treasury on the top bar.

Interface

  • ✚ A new start: the country first, then an archetype or your own character.
  • ✚ The map: Trade, Prices, Wages, Stability, Relations and "Where I can build" modes.
  • ✚ The phone in your hand: it slides up, people call you with requests; Messages, Contacts, and Channels — the world's media.
  • ✚ Themes with their own editor, and icons for every good.

Balance and the rest

  • ✚ Fewer goods stuck at their price cap for years; the world's traders close the price gaps. Buildings cost more: a first billion now takes about 8–12 years instead of 4.
  • ✚ Saves in one place: Documents\My Games\Trade World — every version sees the same saves. The game tells you when a new version is out.
  • ✚ Saves of 0.16.1 load in 0.16.2.
