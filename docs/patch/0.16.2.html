0.16.2 is about computing and energy. The game has a whole new chain: power → data centre → the cloud and tokens. It all runs by the same rules as the rest of the world: cloud and AI companies appear on their dates and compete with you on the same market.

Computing

  • ✚ Data centres: servers are a good made from chips, a data centre holds racks, and the main cost is power; cooling is cheaper in the cold and dearer in the tropics. Your platforms run on your own capacity.
  • ✚ The cloud from 2006: the world's market takes your spare capacity; the price falls every year.
  • ✚ AI models from 2022: train your own model and sell tokens through its API against the world's AI companies. Each generation is four times the work. Thanks to Patient glitch for the idea of tokens as a chain of their own.
  • ✚ The IT office: teams work like a plant's lines. Games are titles of their own: genre, setting, platform, the critics' score, sales, sequels. The world's studios make games by your rules, and the critics get used to good games. The autopilot plans a game for its era and does not start one whose forecast does not pay.
  • ✚ An AI assistant: a new platform with the "Generative AI" node; some of its users pay a subscription.
  • ✚ Servers and fuel from your own source: stations and data centres take fuel and servers from a chosen warehouse or your own well, as a plant's line does.

Experimental: a rival with the brain of your AI model

  • ✚ The phone has a "Rival" app: the magnate closest to you becomes your rival. Connect any model with an OpenAI-compatible address in the settings (Ollama on your own PC, OpenRouter), and once a month the model picks his move against you from the moves magnates have, and he writes to you. You can chat with him, and he remembers. Without a model he plays silently. It is a prototype — tell us how he behaves.

Power and the state

  • ✚ Your own power stations: gas, coal, hydro, solar, wind, nuclear. A station sells to the country's grid at the grid's price.
  • ✚ Power between countries: neighbouring grids trade, the cheap one sells to the dear one, and both treasuries gain. The grid window shows capacity, load, the price per MWh and three years of history.
  • ✚ The country's budget month by month, and a head of state sees the treasury on the top bar.
  • ✚ War: you cannot build in a country at war with yours; a war hits the grid — lost capacity, dearer power, its own line on the grid panel.

Interface

  • ✚ A new start: the country first, then an archetype or your own character; one start sum for everybody.
  • ✚ The map: Trade, Prices, Wages, Stability, Relations and "Where I can build" modes — green, amber, red with the reason on hover. The head of state's portrait on the map, stars for capitals, new badges for your buildings, "Go here" and "Move here" in a city's menu.
  • ✚ The phone in your hand: it slides up, the apps turn in pages; people call you with requests, a talk with someone abroad is a call; Messages, Contacts, and Channels — the world's media with their slant and subscriptions. The phone changes with the era: buttons, colour, smartphone.
  • ✚ Event cards in three tiers of importance; you can drag them.
  • ✚ The goods directory is redone: every good shows where it is used.
  • ✚ Building windows are tidier: labels left of the fields, two columns instead of half-empty windows, the build button always in view.
  • ✚ Themes with their own editor, and icons for every good.

Balance and the rest

  • ✚ Fewer goods stuck at their price cap for years; the world's traders close the price gaps. Buildings cost more and construction loans are leaner: a first billion now takes about 8–12 years instead of 4.
  • ✚ Loopholes closed: a share price catches up with its fair value faster (buying cheap shares no longer makes a billion without a single building); people's startups are no longer worth trillions; an heir's stake is no more than their start money; your exchange lot moves the price by its whole volume.
  • ✚ People who go "their own way" found a company after half a year, as your poorest start does; the export share defaults to 100%; the dynasty tree labels spouses and parents.
  • ✚ Saves in one place: Documents\My Games\Trade World — every version sees the same saves. The game tells you when a new version is out.
  • ✚ Saves of 0.16.1 load in 0.16.2.
