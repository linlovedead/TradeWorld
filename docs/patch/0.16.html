This is the biggest Trade World update so far. The world used to be scenery behind your business. Now it is full of people, each with a character, a memory and goals of their own. Your rivals play by the same rules as you.

Your character grows

  • ✚ Universities. Each one has its own branch (engineering, finance, politics, media), plus an elective that can "mutate" into an unexpected ability.
  • ✚ Thirty perks across five qualities (Management, Intellect, Charisma, Influence, Intrigue). The qualities grow from what you do, not from points on a screen.
  • ✚ A technology tree, 2000–2020. Countries invent smartphones, the cloud, 5G on their own. Your lab can buy samples of a foreign invention and take them apart.

Talks and people

  • ✚ Talk to anyone: a gift, a dinner, a loan, a mentor, a favour, an affair, a threat, compromising material, blackmail, matchmaking for your children — 17 approaches, each showing what its odds are made of.
  • ✚ People come to you: with requests, offers, threats. They have plans of their own: one wants to ruin you, another wants a cut, a third wants to be your friend.
  • ✚ Family. Affairs, love children and their secrets, the dynasty tree. You can cut a child out of the will, but they will remember. The son of a ruined magnate can study, save up and come back with a company of his own.

The world on the map

  • ✚ Logistics. Goods travel for days by road, rail and sea. The straits have owners who can close them, and a blockade cuts off the ports.
  • ✚ Travel. Your first building in a new country is signed in person, so you have to go there.
  • ✚ Electricity is part of the cost of everything made. An overloaded grid gets dearer and shuts plants down.
  • ✚ IT business: an internet provider, a search engine, a social network, video, an online shop, software and operating systems, media as a lever in elections.

A fair world

We checked what you can do against what your rivals can do, and made the rules the same. This is now the core principle of the game: the AI magnates get no shortcuts, and they are no more helpless than you.

  • ✚ No gifts to the AI: companies build plants with their own money, pay permits, taxes and interest, and obey the same laws and bans.
  • ✚ Building takes time, for everyone: a corner shop opens in a month, a small factory in two, a mega-factory in nine. While it is being built, a building does not work and pays no upkeep, and you can set up its goods, shelves and supplies in advance. Companies build their plants by the same rule.
  • ✚ One cap for all: once your stakes in a country's companies are worth half its yearly output, you cannot buy more there, and neither can a magnate. Officials demand their cut from companies too, magnates block other people's bills, and they live on the same money you do.
  • ✚ One set of takeover rules: any target defends against any raider with a buyback, a poison pill, a white knight or a court. Everyone needs 75% to merge.
  • ✚ Magnates play like you: they found their own companies, appoint directors, bring and lobby bills, run for office, use a governor's post, dig dirt on each other and outbid other offers. When they abuse power, the prosecutors come for them as they come for you.

Tutorial and hints

  • ✚ Three new tutorial chapters: “People and ties”, “Study and talents”, “Business abroad”. They walk you through talks, the Circle, universities, trips, logistics, electricity and IT, the way the first chapters walked you through a shop and a plant. Finished the tutorial already? The new chapters are in “Game → Training”.
  • ✚ Opportunity icons at the top, as in Paradox strategies: free talent points, a free research slot, “a university will take you”, a choice at a degree's defence, unattended buildings, someone's scheme against you. A click takes you where it is decided; a right click hides the icon for a month.
  • ✚ Hover hints for the new words: Circle, Schemes, Talents, Electricity and more.
