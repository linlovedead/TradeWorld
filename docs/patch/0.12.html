In 0.11 the portraits showed the back of the head: zoomed out, the map moved the camera so that the face was cut off and you saw the head from the inside. Sorry about that. 0.12 fixes it, and we rebuilt the faces while we were at it.

What's new

  • ✚ Painted portraits. A painter's light, brush strokes, a backdrop behind the head — instead of plastic 3D.
  • ✚ More different people. 107 facial features instead of 41: nose, lips, eyelids, ears, chin, a touch of asymmetry.
  • ✚ Age in the face. Wrinkles, folds, jowls; grey hair is no longer the only sign of age.
  • ✚ Skin. Stubble, freckles, moles, blush, make-up.
  • ✚ Hair and clothes. 40 hairstyles instead of 10, women's business suits and blouses, new men's suits.
  • ✚ Characters in full height — in the Character window, on a person's card and in the face editor.

It is the same generator: everyone has their own "DNA", children look like their parents, people age with the world. Only the brush has changed.

The figure is the first step of the character branch: relations between people in the spirit of Crusader Kings, traits and travel around the world are ahead.
